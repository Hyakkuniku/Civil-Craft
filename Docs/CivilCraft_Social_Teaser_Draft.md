**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920  
**Placement:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

## Voiceover script

> Think it'll hold?
>
> Build your design.
>
> Put it to the test.
>
> Learn from every attempt. Make the next one stronger.
>
> Civil Craft. Build. Test. Learn.

Read conversationally, with a curious opening and a confident ending. Leave the collapse beat silent so the sound effect has space. The text and gameplay should also tell the story when watched without sound. Caption the spoken lines, and avoid stacking a large headline over a large subtitle.

## Footage to capture

1. A weak bridge failing under a vehicle load, including the vehicle's approach.
2. The same bridge site in Build Mode: road placement and truss construction.
3. A test with the existing structural stress display visible.
4. One clear reinforcement edit and a successful crossing in the next test.
5. A clean view of the finished bridge, canyon, and player.
6. The official game logo for the end card.

Capture a few extra seconds before and after each action. Keep the complete span and vehicle readable in the vertical composition; if a wide bridge will not fit a crop, place the landscape gameplay inside a designed vertical frame. Keep headlines, subtitles, and the logo away from the screen edges and social app controls.

In the Unity Editor or a PC build, the existing **Ctrl+H** shortcut toggles the gameplay HUD for clean footage. Keep relevant build controls or stress feedback visible in the shots that demonstrate those features.
