# Civil Craft story NPCs

## Map 1 · CanyonCrossing

| Order | NPC | Story role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Professor Bhan | Teaches Beam Bridge and Truss Bridge, introduces Canyonville, and sends the player to Reed. |
| 2 | Grandma Susan (Lola) | Runs the Shop and introduces it during Bhan’s Canyonville sequence. The player returns to Bhan afterward. |
| 3 | Reed | Introduces bending and gives the factory crossing contract. |
| 4 | Vance | Teaches tension and compression. Cargo loading is part of his main contract. |
| 5 | Silas | Gives the Load Distribution and Load Transfer contracts, unlocks Multiplayer, and leads into Town River. |

## Map 2 · Town River / Rivermere

| Order | NPC | Story role |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Marsh | Introduces concrete materials and arch bridges. |
| 7 | Ford | Gives the construction-site crossing and teaches material stress. |
| 8 | Brooks | Gives the market crossing and teaches moving loads. The taxi crosses before the cargo truck. |
| 9 | Ronan | Gives the southern crossing and teaches shear. His farewell points the player toward Ironhaven. |

## Map 3 · Ironhaven

| Order | NPC | Planned main contracts |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Clark | Freight Passage → The Offset Road |
| 11 | Mills | Heavy Equipment Route → Workers’ Crossing → Reconnecting Arcadia |

## Optional contracts

- **Reed** and **Vance** already have their inspection and realignment side contracts, respectively.
- **Marsh, Ford, Brooks, and Ronan** have optional side contracts on the progression checklist.
- **Clark** has Night Delivery and Maintenance Access in the design.
- **Mills** has Crane Transfer and Emergency Industrial Route in the design.

See the [NPC progression checklist](NPC-Progression-Checklist.md) and [Ironhaven reference](References/Map3-Ironhaven/Reference-notes.md) for remaining work and contract details.
